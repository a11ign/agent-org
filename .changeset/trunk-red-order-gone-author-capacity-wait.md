A `trunk-red` order for an author who has ended, refused only because every engineer seat is `working` or holds its one row, is a DEFERRED capacity wait under the half-hour limit and no longer a fault from its first tick (a11ign/a11ign#3814). `routeWithFallback` writes `no workspace labelled "<author>"; and the fallback "engineers": no engineer is idle ...`, which the anchored `CAPACITY_REFUSAL` did not match, so the order was `UNDELIVERED` and counted as `nowhere to go` for 30 ticks on 2026-10-06. Only an ABSENT author and only the `engineers` fallback are accepted; an idle seat, a `; no spawn:` tail, an author that is merely `working`, or any other fallback stays a fault.
