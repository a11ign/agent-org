The tick's cost line breaks the gate's one `node work-gate.ts` call into its five largest spans, `gateSpans` (a11ign/agent-org#1050). The gate was ONE entry in `phaseCalls` and its own `gh` calls sat in `subcommands` with every other phase's, so 78 to 139 s of gate wall (300 s at the budget since #1082) could not be cut by what it spends: 534 `gh` calls and 181 s were `other`, and the read the gate was killed in was a `node -e` the census cannot name. `work-gate.ts`'s `main` now ends a span at each step through `endGateSpan(name, value)` (`lib/spawn-census.ts`), which returns its value untouched and appends `{ name, wallMs, cpuMs, ghCalls }` to the file the tick names in the gate's environment (`AGENT_ORG_GATE_SPANS`, the gate's alone). The first span begins at the process's start and each begins where the last ended, so the spans sum to the call. The tick reads the file after the gate, keeps the five largest wall first, and writes `gateSpans: null` (never `[]`) for a gate that reported none. A gate killed at its budget adds one span, `unfinished after <last step>`, for the wall the spans that ended do not account for, with no CPU or `gh` count. Lines that are not spans are ignored, and the gate's stdout and stderr are unchanged.
