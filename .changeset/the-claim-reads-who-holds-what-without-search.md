The claim's label reads no longer go through GitHub's SEARCH (a11ign#5039). `gh issue list --label X` is sent by `gh` as a search, whose allowance is separate from, and far smaller than, the GraphQL points: on 2026-10-11 the scheduler App's installation spent it and every claim was refused with `B4 COULD NOT BE ASKED which rows are already claimed` (eighteen times in thirty minutes) while 3,948 points were left. `lookupClaimedRegions` (B4's read of who holds what), the sweep freeze's second look at the same list, the held-rows read (B2), the chairman-rows read and the shared-failure incident rows now read `repository.issues(labels:)`, a connection that the search allowance does not govern, through one reader, `src/ticket-port/rows-by-label.ts`. A failed read is still `null` and still INCONCLUSIVE, never "nobody holds a file". A read refused for a rate limit is remembered for the process (the tick) and not asked again, and the line it leaves names the resource: the refused response's own `X-Ratelimit-Resource`, else one follow-up read of the GraphQL pool. `src/packaging/no-search-reads.test.ts` fails on a `issue list` / `pr list` argv carrying `--label` or `--search` in the files the claim reads through. The gate's own label reads and the per-tick volume cut are the follow-up row, after #5032 merges.
