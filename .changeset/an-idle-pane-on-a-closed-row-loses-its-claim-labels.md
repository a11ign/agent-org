An idle or done `worker-<n>` pane no longer holds a closed row's claim labels for ever. `holdsClosedRow` held every listed `worker-<n>` in any status ("it is mid-turn on the row it just closed", #3883), but a pane between turns stays listed, and `spareDecision` ends a finished spare only when no OPEN row carries its `session:` label, so the closed row kept the label and the pane and its slot with it (15 worker panes, none working, 10 idle and 5 done, each on a closed row, measured 2026-10-10). A `worker-<n>` now holds a closed row while herdr reports it anywhere but idle or done, and for 10 minutes after the close when it is idle or done; past that the row's `in-progress`, `started` and `session:*` come off and the teardown ends the pane. A missing, unparseable or future `closedAt` still holds, and a standing seat's day is unchanged. The closed-label read also asks every declared tracker, not the home one only, and the strip acts in the repository the row is in; a tracker whose read is refused is named on stderr and the others' rows are still decided (`rows` is `null` only when every read was refused). a11ign/a11ign#4437; a11ign/agent-org#745.
