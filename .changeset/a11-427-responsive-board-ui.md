The board UI works on a tablet and a phone (A11-427). At 1024px and below the sidebar gives way to a sticky top bar: the mark, the tabs Tickets / Runs / Settings, the notification bell, the instance chip and the search icon. At 640px and below the tabs are a bottom tab bar with the number of runs running on Runs, and the tab bar steps aside while a sheet or a dialog is open. Tickets offers its projects and goals as a Project and a Goal chip that open one dialog (a bottom sheet on a phone), Runs keeps its status filter as a strip of pills under the bar, and Settings its sections. Every control takes a 44px tap, a text field is 16px on a phone, rows become cards, New ticket, Review changes, the plugin confirms and Run options become sheets, and no page scrolls sideways. A desktop wider than 1024px is unchanged.
