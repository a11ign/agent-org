A routing is ONE counted decision in the decision log, whichever shape wrote its lines (a11ign/a11ign#4754, use 1 of #4627). `decide` wrote a request line with no `outcome` and `recordOutcome` wrote the line that had one, so 75 of the 161 `model-routing` lines read `outcome: None` and a reader counting by outcome counted each routing twice. The request line now carries `outcome: "asked"` (nothing has come of it yet), so every line has an `outcome`; the two lines stay two, because `class-match` records a human's keep or removal days later in another process and a single line written at outcome time would leave nothing for a decision that never got one. `decisionsIn(lines)` is the one reading that counts a routing once over the old shape (a request with no `outcome`) and the new alike, pairing by `use`, `id` and file order; an outcome line with no request (an override, a use switched off) is a routing of its own, and a request never closed reads `asked`. Lines already in the log are not rewritten and still parse, `readAnswer` and the request line's `answers` are untouched, and the header of `decision-provider.ts` names the two line shapes. A reader that took `outcome !== undefined` to mean the outcome line must now also check `answers === undefined` (`engineer-route.test.ts` did, and says so).
