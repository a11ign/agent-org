A change to the org's own machinery now ships behind a flag the gate watches for an hour, and every suppression fails open in 15 minutes (a11ign/a11ign#5100). Four of the chairman's 2026-10-11 regressions were machinery fixes that were green in tests and broken live. `pr:open` refuses a pull request that touches the gate, claims, the tick, waking, arming, releases or identities unless its body carries `Flag: <name>` (or `Flag: none -- <reason>`), `Health-signals:` (one to three of `claims-refused`, `blind-ticks`, `wakes-held`, `approved-unqueued`, `incidents-not-real`, `rate-limit-refusals`, `release-failures`) and `Revert:`. The gate samples those signals every tick into `machinery-canary.json` in its state directory; the tick that finds a flag on starts its hour, and a signal whose count since reaches twice the hour before (and 3 more) turns the flag OFF, files an `incident` row carrying both readings and orders `ceo`. A signal no tick could read is UNKNOWN, never clean. A GitHub-incident hold on a red pull request is handed to its owner, with the reason and the original order, after 15 minutes unless a claimed row is working it (`expireSuppressions`, which the other suppressors can call). Options, on by default, are the `machineryCanary` key of `host.json`: `enabled`, `windowMinutes`, `degradeFactor`, `minRise`, `suppressionMinutes`; a mistyped one is a logged problem and the defaults stand.
