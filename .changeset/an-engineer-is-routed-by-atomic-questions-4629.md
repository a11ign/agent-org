A new engineer's model and effort are routed per row (`src/engineer-route.ts`, called from the tick before delivery and read by `tierOfRow` in `wake.ts`). Five atomic questions (mechanical, across subsystems, debugging an unknown failure, does the Acceptance cover the Done-when, a 1 to 5 complexity score) go through `decide` under the `model-routing` use, and the pure `composeRoute` turns the answers into Haiku/high, Sonnet/medium or Sonnet/high. With no provider, no key, the use off, a refusal, a timeout or an answer under the floor, a Region of at most 3 files with a command Acceptance gives Sonnet/medium and anything else Sonnet/high; the fallback never picks Haiku. A `tier:haiku` label and a row `tier:haiku` would refuse (`lane:ceo`, `needs:chairman`, a `.github/workflows/` Region, no command Acceptance) are decided before the provider is asked, and `haiku-tier.json` still stops Haiku starts. Every route is a decision-log line. a11ign/a11ign#4629.
