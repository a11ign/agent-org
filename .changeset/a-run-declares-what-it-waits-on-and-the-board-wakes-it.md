A worker's `working` outcome may declare what it waits on: `waitingOn` as a list of `{ fleetJob }`, `{ ciRun }`, `{ pullRequest, until: merged|reviewed }` and `{ ticket, until: done }`, with `until` as the deadline. The board reads each event itself (a fleet job through `fleet job status <id> --json`, a CI run and a pull request through the code host, a ticket through the tracker), at most once a minute per run, only while the agent is idle, and prompts the worker's pane ONCE when all have finished or any has failed, saying what happened (a job's state and results path, a run's conclusion, the reason first on a failure). Past `until` with events still pending it prompts once with their progress, and marks the run Stalled after `tickets.stallMinutes` more of idleness. The card reads `Waiting on fleet job fj-1 (4 of 6 pages, about 20 min)`, never Stalled, for a declared wait inside its deadline. A worker woken for an ended wait that declares the same items again is marked Stalled and not woken. The stop hook lets a declared `working` stop with no shell or Monitor running, and the worker brief and the shipped house notes say to declare the wait, end the turn and write no wait loop. A11-236's undeclared wait (`waitingOn` as text, held by the worker's own shell) now gets the same one prompt at its bound before it reads Stalled. `OUTCOME_EXAMPLES.working` is the declared form, and a `working` outcome's `until` is now checked as a full ISO time on a real day (a bare date is refused).
