A re-review that stays not convinced at an unchanged head is no longer lost and no longer ends with a person (A11-290). The verdict door keeps a copy of a verdict it refuses for an equal patch (`refused-<file>`, beside the reviewer's own), and the board logs the re-review on the run as `rereview: still not convinced` with the reasons and comments the result on the pull request (code host contract 1.25, `commentOnPull`). It then routes the stand-off once for each head: to the planner first when the worker's reply disputes a decision of the plan or the ticket, or when the ticket's Decisions changed and summoned the re-review that stayed not convinced (the changed Decision is quoted to the planner at once), which amends the decision in the plan (the reviewer then re-reviews bound by the amendment, reviewer contract 1.10, `rereview.planDecisions`) or upholds it, and then to the worker, whose round carries the re-reviewer's reasons and ends only with a change: the Stop hook refuses a second reply with no change at the same head unless the worker stops on an item of the escalation list. The A11-213 flag fires only after both routes, and its card says what the reviewer, the worker and the planner last said. `/api/queue` carries a `health` series of person-interventions and stand-offs settled with no person.
