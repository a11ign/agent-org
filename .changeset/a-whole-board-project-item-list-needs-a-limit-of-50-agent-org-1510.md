`host/gh` refuses `gh project item-list` unless `--limit` is 50 or less. A whole-board listing spends about 52 points of the GraphQL pool every manager shares, and the wrapper let it through unpriced: its ledger line read `graphql?`, so the ledger could not say what the call cost. A call with no `--limit`, or with one over 50 (in every spelling, `--limit N`, `--limit=N`, `-L N`, `-LN`), now exits 1 before gh-real and before the ledger, so it writes no line, and names the helper that reads one row (`agent-org row-file --promote=<n>`) or the whole board (`board-snapshot`). An allowed listing is priced: its line is `graphql` with the pool's counter after the call, read from the debug head `GH_DEBUG=api` makes gh-real print for the listing alone, and that head is taken out of the caller's stderr. Other `gh project`, `issue`, `pr` and `api` calls are unchanged. agent-org#1510.
