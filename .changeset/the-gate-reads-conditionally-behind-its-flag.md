The gate's polling reads go through the ETag/304 reader and the tick holds the GraphQL budget, behind `gate.restConditional` (a11ign/a11ign#5169, the wiring of #5128; the building blocks are agent-org#1096). With the marker present, `readPrs` and `readOpenRows` ask GitHub's REST list with the stored `If-None-Match` and answer a `304` from the kept body; a pull request whose head sha and `updatedAt` are unchanged is not re-read, unless a check on it is still running or the kept detail is ten minutes old (neither moves `updated_at`). A list that would cost no more than viewing its changed members is read whole, as it always was. The tick, when it is the gate and the flag is on, holds `tickBudget` (`TICK_POINTS_CAP`), charges every GraphQL read made through `defaultRun`, the capturing runner and `runBatch`, cuts back a cause already past half the cap once three quarters is spent, and writes `gate-budget:` and `gate-rest:` lines on exit for the live hour. The reads are selected by whether their `run` stands for the gate's own `gh` (`standingFor`), which holds through the first wave's rehearsal and replay wrappers, so `readTrackerLanes`, `readOtherScopes` and the keyed lanes read conditionally and a test's `run` never does. Flag off, nothing here runs and the reads are the ones the gate has always made; revert is deleting the marker. Every failure of the conditional path is said on stderr and answered by the read as it was; a refusal is `null`, never `[]` (#1286), and the first-wave rehearsal's `[]` is never kept.
