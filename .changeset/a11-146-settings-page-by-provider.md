The Settings page groups its settings by provider: one collapsible section for each provider and one for the conventions plugin, with the machine's plumbing in a `Machine` section that starts closed. Each setting is one line that says whether it is `Editable` or `Read-only`, its explanation sits behind a `Details` disclosure, and a search box filters the settings by key or description. Editing is as before.
