Each After-merge check is sent to its runner, and one left open a week is sent again, once, overdue (A11-277, step 3 of 3). `GET /api/session-asks` is the person's session's queue: it lists every open check the session runs as `kind: "after-merge-check"`, with its ticket, its line, who runs it, what evidence would close it (the plan's `Evidence:`, or one line saying what was seen and where), when it was sent, whether it is overdue, and the route that closes it. A check is sent the first time the queue lists it, and `?since=<at>` from an earlier read lists only what was sent since. A person check on an escalation item is never sent to the session: it waits under "Needs you" at the top of the Runs page's After-merge list, with the item's label. A check open 7 days after it was sent (to the session, or shown to the person under Needs you), or after the board began to watch it, is marked overdue once and never again; a person's check on no escalation item now reads "the session", which runs it; a session check is sent again, and a board check nobody saw pass goes to the session and stays watched. The escalation rule every brief quotes now says an After-merge check the session runs is not a stop, and names the queue and the close route.
