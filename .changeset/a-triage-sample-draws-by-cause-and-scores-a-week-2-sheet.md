`node src/trace/triage-sample.ts --cause <class>` (repeatable) restricts the triage sample to the named cause classes, and refuses a name no manager wake in the window carries rather than drawing an empty sample. `--score <predictions.json> --labels <labels.json>` scores a labelled week-2 sheet against a stricter bar: a class passes only with at least 20 labelled `wake` rows and no missed wake, and under 20 is reported as "not testable this window", a result and not a failure. Without `--labels` the frozen #4074 sheet is scored against the existing bar exactly as before. a11ign/a11ign#4187.
