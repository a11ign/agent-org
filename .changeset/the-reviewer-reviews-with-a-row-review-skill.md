The reviewer can review with a `row-review` skill (agent-org#1268): the Spec item table, Tests and correctness, and Standards, with the verdict rule that a partial, missing or deferred Done-when item, an unexplained out-of-Region path, an untested changed behaviour, a correctness defect or a duplicated helper is `not convinced`. `skills/row-review/` ships in this repository (the adaptation of mattpocock/skills `code-review`, MIT, with its `LICENSE`), `host:install` copies it to `~/.codex/skills/row-review`, and `host:check` reports it NOT INSTALLED or STALE. The reviewer's order names it only behind the machinery flag `review.rowReviewSkill` (OFF by default; `agent-org machinery:flag on review.rowReviewSkill --session=<you>`). With the flag off the order is the old one with one sentence removed: it asked for "one comment" where the reviewer role file says to post none. `node scripts/review-eval.ts --set blind-2026-10-12 --require 9/11` re-runs the skill over the eleven labelled pull requests (five `not convinced`, six `convinced`) with the reviewer's own model and effort, posts nothing, and exits 1 unless at least nine agree and every `not convinced` does.
