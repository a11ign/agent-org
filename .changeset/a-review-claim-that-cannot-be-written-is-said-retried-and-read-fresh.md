A review claim GitHub refuses to take no longer blocks reviews silently or for good. `agent-org doctor` now asks the `reviewer` App (which writes the claim) for its "Commit statuses" write permission and the `scheduler` App (which lists it) for read, by reading each App's installation, and names the exact permission to grant when one lacks it (`commit statuses reviewer`, `commit statuses scheduler`). A claim that fails is not recorded as started: the next sweep tries the head again and `ticket:review` can start it once the claim succeeds, its pull request's line reads `cannot claim for review: <GitHub's reason>`, and the board shows one warning for all such pull requests. An instance that is the only one (no other instance's `agent-org/review` status seen on any head within the lease) starts the reviewer anyway when the write was refused after a clean read, and the board says `reviewing without a claim`; with a second instance in sight it does not. The claim's read now goes past the `gh` wrapper's read cache, so a claim written as one App is seen at once when read as another, and `onboard` retires the hand-set `gh-read-cache-off.conf` drop-in, which `doctor` fails while it stands. The reviewer plugin contract is 1.2: `start`'s answer may carry `unclaimed`.
