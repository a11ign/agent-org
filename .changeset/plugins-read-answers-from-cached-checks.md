`GET /api/plugins` answers at once from the plugins' last checks, with `checkedAt` (when they finished, null before the first) and `checking`, and runs them again behind the page when they are older than a minute, the host file changed, an action was taken, or the read asks with `?check` (the page's Retry and "Read the plugins again"). The board starts the checks at its start, so the first read normally finds them. Each plugin's checks, and the messaging plugin's, run in a child process of their own, all at once, and a plugin whose checks have not finished within 10 seconds is one failing row ("its checks did not finish within 10 seconds"); the child and the programs it started are stopped. The checks synchronously run `security`, `claude mcp list` and `gh`, which held the board's whole process still: on a loaded host every Settings page waited about 9 s, and its other reads timed out. The Plugins page says how old the checks are under its header ("Checked 2 min ago", "· checking again…", or "Checking the plugins…" with every card unknown until the first finish), reads again every 2 s while they run, and the attention badge counts only checks that have finished. `agent-org doctor` runs the checks as before. `node scripts/page-timing.ts [--connection <name>] [--runs <n>]` times each page's reads through the page's service path. A11-270.
