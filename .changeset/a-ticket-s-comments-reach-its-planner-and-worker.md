A ticket's comments reach its planner and its worker. The briefs carried the ticket's title and description only, so a worker whose ticket had a later comment naming the real cause, and saying it superseded the description, re-raised the theory the comment had ruled out. The tracker contract is 1.8: the optional `readComments(key)` gives every comment with its author and time, and the Linear plugin reads them a page at a time, at most ten pages, leaving out the board's claim records. The planning, batch planning and work briefs carry every comment a person wrote, oldest first, with its author and date, say that a later comment can correct or supersede the description, and ask the plan (`## Comments acted on`) or the pull request body (`Comments acted on`) to name the comments acted on and those set aside. The board's own completion, After-merge, closed-work, put-back and wait-cycle comments are left out. A thread past 30 comments or 60,000 characters keeps its newest, and says how many earlier ones it left out, from when to when, and where to read them; a comment past 6,000 characters is cut with the cut said. A tracker that refuses the read, or has no `readComments`, is said in the brief, and the run still starts.
