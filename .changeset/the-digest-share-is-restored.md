The wake-triage digest share is restored where the ledger says it was lost (a11ign/a11ign#4878, the chairman's reading of a11ign#4627, 2026-10-10: wake 217, digest 1 in six hours, 0.5%, against 9.6% on 10-09). Two causes, read from `~/.cache/a11ign/triage-digest` and not guessed, and one thing this change does not do.

**1. A red main in ANY repository woke every manager order unasked, for 4 h 16 min.** `wake.ts` hands the route `mainRed: withStalls.some(namesRedMain)`, a fact about the tick, and `stateOf` gave it to every order; `triageOrder` wakes an order whose `mainRed` is true BEFORE asking (`via: none`, no `answers`). From 12:35:51Z to 16:51:41Z on 2026-10-10 a red `lab` main (`trunk-red` for `pr-lab#61` through `#75`, each already routed to its fixer) did that to all 197 manager orders of the window: 197 of 197 `via: none` lines fall inside it, and none outside it. `names-red-main` asks whether THIS event names a red main, so `stateOf` now tells an order `mainRed` only when it is itself the red main's order (`namesRedMain`). Every other order of the tick is asked as it would be on a green main, and is still held, never dropped.

**2. The provider was unsure of two of the five questions, and the ledger could not say which.** Measured 2026-10-10 over 30 recent orders asked with the plain `yes`/`no` criteria: `repeat`, `names-red-main` and `names-chairman-direction` answered at 1.0 and never fell back, while `asks-this-seat` fell under the 0.7 floor on 16 of 30 (mean 0.49) and `informational-only` on 21 of 30 (mean 0.41). The `asked` line kept only the weakest confidence, so which question was under the floor was unreadable. It now keeps `readings`, what the provider said of each question it answered and at what confidence, beside `answers` (the value used, a fallback when the reading was under the floor).

**What changes for the two unsure questions.** They are sent as structured options (`what`, `not_for`, `examples`, the shape agent-org#564 and #4752 use) whose examples are the one set of orders whose answer is known: the frozen sheet of a11ign/a11ign#4074 (94 orders the product-manager labelled on 2026-10-08), every `(cause, seat)` it labels one way and only one way at least twice (12 `wake`, 7 `digest`). A test re-derives both lists from the sheet and fails on a pair added, dropped or miscounted. **The floor is unchanged, no question carries one of its own, and `compose` is unchanged.**

**Measured, and the limits of it** (the live provider, the facts the tick sends and no order text; counts only):
- The sheet's 87 `wake`/`digest` orders, structured criteria against plain: 0 of 51 `wake`-labelled orders digested under either (no false digest); `digest`-labelled orders digested 11 of 36 against 2 of 36. 10 of the 11 are on pairs the examples themselves name, so that is in-sample, and only 1 of the 13 `digest` orders outside them was digested: the criteria do not generalise to a cause with no example. Under the floor: `asks-this-seat` 40 of 87 against 62, `informational-only` 42 of 87 against 67.
- The last 150 live orders: digested 7 against 5 (4.7% against 3.3%); `informational-only` under the floor 91 of 150 against 121, `asks-this-seat` 98 against 95 (not better).
- **So the criteria alone do not reach the 5% line, and this change does not claim they do.** Of 625 live orders of the five-question era, 38 (6.1%) are on a pair the sheet labels `digest` unanimously at least twice, so about 6% is the ceiling the sheet sets, and the provider reaches little of it while `asks-this-seat` stays unsure on the causes it has no example for. The digest still never drops an order: every held order rides its seat's next order or is flushed alone at 60 minutes, and the tests that pin both are unchanged.

`wake.ts` still passes the tick-wide `mainRed` and its comment above that line still says it is asked of every order; that file is outside this change's Region, so the stale comment and the redundant conjunction are left for a follow-up row.
