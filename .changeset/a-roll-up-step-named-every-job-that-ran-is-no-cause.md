The shared-failure detector reads the `gate` roll-up's own step as no cause in its other spelling too: `Every job that ran, ran clean` is matched as well as `Every job ran, and ran clean`, so a red roll-up whose check is not named `gate` carries no signature. Until now only the first spelling was excluded by step name. a11ign/agent-org#968.
