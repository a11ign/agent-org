A reply to the reviewer's `changes requested` review now gets one re-review of the same head, with no new commit. When the board's own account (the run's worker) or a person the org declares answers the review in a comment, a review, or a review-thread comment, the pool starts the reviewer once more for that head, with the replies in its prompt; the head is recorded in `reviewers.json` (`rereviewed`) before the start, so it happens once however many replies follow. The board says `re-review asked by reply at <head>`. An approval that names the standing review clears the block; a re-review that stays `not convinced` is refused by the verdict door at an equal patch, as before, and is not read as a verdict that failed to post. Every ticket-mode reviewer prompt, the first review's and the reprompt's included, now carries the ticket's `## Done-when` section (from the run that holds the pull request) and the rule that a Done-when line under "After merge" is not a merge condition: the review checks that the pull request names how and by whom it will be run. The code host contract goes to 1.2 (`readReplies`) and the reviewer contract to 1.1 (`start` and `reprompt` take a brief); the bundled GitHub and atomic plugins are at those versions. The review claim lets a re-review claim a head after its verdict.
