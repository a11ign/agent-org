A ticket-mode worker ends its round at the push and no longer waits on CI (A11-306). Workers held a run open 10 to 25 minutes after pushing, on `gh pr checks --watch` or a `sleep` loop, and runs idled between a green head and its review. The worker brief, every review round's steps and the shipped house notes now say: push, write the outcome file, end, and do not wait on CI or the review. The board wakes the run on a red required check (a failure the infrastructure caused is rerun first) and on review feedback, as it did. The Bash hook refuses `gh pr checks … --watch`, `gh run watch` and a `sleep` over 60 seconds, with a message that the board wakes the run; the Stop hook is as it was. The reviewer for a head with no review now starts within 2 minutes of its required checks going green: the board looks at the open pull requests' reviewers every minute (`REVIEW_START_MS`), where the rest of its watch is every 3, and logs how long after the checks were first read green each reviewer started, saying so when that is past 2 minutes. A machine that wrote its own `tickets.brief` notes keeps them; the shipped text changes only where no file exists.
