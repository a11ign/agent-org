A stand-off is no longer recorded or routed on a sweep that could not read the worker's replies (A11-290, follow-up to the stand-off route). Replies that cannot be read are not "no reply": whether the worker disputed a decision, which only the planner can settle, is unknown, so the board holds the refused re-review's result and asks again on the next sweep instead of sending the stand-off to the worker.
