A ticket the board asks the writer for on its own (a Dependabot major or a red update group) is filed and shown Done instead of Failed (A11-267). The dependency sweep reads the team of its epic A11-251 from the tracker each sweep and sets it on the writer run and in the request, and asks for nothing while the epic cannot be read. A new-ticket brief has one ordered Team rule in the same words for every run: the request's own team, else the form's parent; the team `tickets.pick` names; the workspace's only team; and needs-human only when none is left. The Stop hook judges a new-ticket run by the writer's outcomes (`created`, `decided`, `needs-human`), so a writer that filed its ticket can record it; a ticket worker still cannot end on `created`, and a writer cannot end on a worker's outcome.
