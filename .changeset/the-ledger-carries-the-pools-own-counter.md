The `host/gh` ledger carries the pool's own counter, so `api-pool-low`'s Spender sentence accounts for the pool and not for the floor. Measured 2026-10-13 for `a11ign-scheduler[bot]` (19:08Z to 20:08Z): the pool went from full to 5 of 5000 and the ledger held 1,557 graphql lines for it, none with a `cost` (a response names its cost only when the query asked `rateLimit { cost }`, and `gh pr list` and `gh issue view` build their own), so every line read as the one-point floor: about 30%. An `api graphql` call is now asked with `-i`; the response head's `X-Ratelimit-Used` and `-Reset` are written as the line's 11th and 12th fields and the head is taken out of the copy before the caller reads it. `settle` (`src/gh-ledger.ts`) reads the counter's movement between two readings as the points spent between them: a call whose reading it has is `read`, calls it has no reading for (`pr list`, `issue view`) share the movement with the reading that closed it (`shared`, an inference, said as one), and what no ledgered call accounts for is `unplaced`. The sentence says which: `4900 points by the pool's own counter: 3200 read off it, 1699 shared between calls it was not read for, 1 floor`. A ledger with no counter reads as it did, a floor, and says so. The wrapper's pinned digest in `host-project-paths.test.ts` moves with it.
