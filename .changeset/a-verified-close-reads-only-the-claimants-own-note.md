The gate orders a row closed on the claimant's own VERIFIED note only. A comment by another seat on the shared account (marked `decided-by` for another seat) is not that note, and neither is one that says NOT closing or a Done-when item NOT met, so a note refusing the close no longer orders it closed (a11ign/agent-org#1596; a11ign#5311).
