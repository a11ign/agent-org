A `priority:chairman` row is no longer refused by B4 at the claim for a holder that is not itself a chairman row, so the row the gate offers is the row the claim accepts. The gate's `overlapVerdict` already yielded (the chairman's direction on a11ign/a11ign#4524) while `row-claim` refused the same row for the same overlap, so #4764 was offered and refused six ticks running. `sessionEligibility` asks B4's two halves over every holder as before and, only when they refuse, reads whether the CHAIRMAN labelled the row (the gate's own `readChairmanPriority` over the tracker's list: the newest `labeled` event's login must be one of `CHAIRMAN_LOGINS`; an unreadable history fails closed to today's refusal). A chairman row then asks B4 again over the chairman holders alone, so two chairman rows still exclude each other; every other check (B2, the `blockedBy` edge, drain, one row per instance, the sweep freeze, a Region or claimed list that could not be read) is unchanged, and a row without the label is refused exactly as before. A claim that goes ahead names the holders it walked past on stderr and in a comment on the row. `sessionEligibilityReason` keeps its signature and is the new `sessionEligibility`'s `reason`. a11ign/a11ign#4793.
