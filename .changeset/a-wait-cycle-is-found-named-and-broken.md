The board finds and breaks every wait cycle on the tick (A11-272, step 2 of 3). A ticket, a run's blockers, the wait its idle worker declared and the files it holds are read as one graph (`board-view/wait-cycles.ts`): on 2026-10-25 A11-231 waited on A11-229 while A11-229 was held behind A11-231's files, and nothing saw it. A run in a cycle whose worker declared a ticket wait on another ticket of it is now let go at once, without waiting for the wait's first reading: the tracker records the blocker, its workspace closes, and it is queued behind the ticket with its session. A cycle the board cannot break (blockers alone, a pull request wait, a batch's run) is named and left. The chairman's session hears of each cycle once, as one tracker comment on its smallest key (recorded in `tickets/wait-cycles.json`, tried again if the tracker refuses it, and told again when a cycle clears and forms again). The card of a run parked on a wait now has the status Waiting, never In progress, and says what it waits on and until when (`Waiting on ticket A11-229 (In Progress), until 16:30`); a ticket in a cycle names it on its card, and the Runs page shows one banner for each cycle. `scripts/check-wait-state.ts` runs the ticket's After-merge check over a queue.
