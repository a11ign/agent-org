The board no longer loses Linear within an hour of a restart (A11-359; A11-345's one retry on `Connection: close` did not hold, and while Linear was lost no queued run started). Every call to Linear, a query, a mutation, a plan download or an upload, now opens a connection of its own and closes it after (`src/fresh-fetch.ts`), so a keep-alive socket that Linear closed while the loop was busy can never be reused and reset. A call that fails to reach Linear, runs past 10 s, or is answered 429 or 5xx is tried again on a new connection after 200 ms, 800 ms and 2 s, up to three times. `fill()` no longer gives up in silence: each read it makes (blockers, the heads of stacked blockers, plan paths, the host's preflight) has a time of its own (`readMs`, a minute), a ticket whose read fails or times out is held with its reason on its card (`blockersFailure`, `pathsFailure`) while the other queued runs start, a failed project read keeps the repository a run last had, a failed refresh keeps the blockers each run last had, and a pass that starts nothing says why in the log, once for each reason. The board logs a line with its failure rate for every failed call, and `agent-org doctor` has a `linear calls` row that fails when more than 1 call in 100 failed over the last hour.
