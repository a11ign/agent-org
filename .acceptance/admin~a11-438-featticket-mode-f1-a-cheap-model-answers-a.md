A Needs-you question answered before a person is asked (A11-438, step 1 of 2), behind `tickets.autoAnswer`, off by default. `src/ticket-mode/auto-answer.ts` decides which questions the answerer may take (none on the list, or `reserved` or `contradicts`), reads the sources (the ticket, its parent, their files, the run's repositories' AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md and README.md, the other tickets' Decisions), asks the model and decides at `tickets.autoAnswerThreshold` (0.9). `src/ticket-mode/pool-auto-answer.ts` holds the run while the model is asked, then sends the answer to the run and records it (`Entry.autoAnswer`, a run-log `auto-answered` event), or parks the run as before. The answer route (`pool-answer.ts`) takes a person's answer that overrules an automatic one while the run goes on. The tally counts `autoAnswers` and `personAnswers` apart. The run's card shows the answer with its sources.
